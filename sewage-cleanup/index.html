Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. When it happens in Baldwin Park, Baldwin Park Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
